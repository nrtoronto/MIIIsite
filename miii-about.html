About MIII Holdings

We build the infrastructure
that builds companies.

MIII Holdings is a Toronto-based diversified holding company operating at the intersection of technology, media, and creative culture. We don't just invest — we build, operate, and compound.

Our Story

Not a fund.
Not an agency.
An operating company.

MIII Holdings was founded on a single conviction: the most durable competitive advantage is not a product, not a team, not even timing — it is infrastructure that compounds over time.

Where traditional holding companies acquire and manage, MIII builds from first principles. Every venture in the portfolio is conceived, constructed, and scaled on shared AI-native rails — autonomous agent loops, persistent intelligence systems, and modular workflows that grow without proportional headcount.

We operate across five verticals simultaneously: Media, Service, Technology, Retail, and MIII Studios. Each division is distinct in focus but unified in infrastructure — the same operating system powering a music label roster also drives founder discovery, content publishing, and deal flow across the entire portfolio.
